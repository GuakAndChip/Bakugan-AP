# Bakugan Archipelago Client (BakuganAP)

Standalone Windows client for the Bakugan Battle Brawlers apworld. Hooks Dolphin with pure ctypes, tracks stats, sends location checks and the goal, and writes received items into the game.

## Bakugan AP

- **Checks**: Defeat enemies, win tournament medals/crests, rank milestones, achievements, and the two collector milestones.
- **Goals**: Story (Beat the Main Game and earn the Darkus Crest), Tournament (Win a tournament of your choosing. Neo Challengers is the fastest), Rank 1 (Become rank 1 on the brawler ranking board, about 400,000RP).
- **Items**: Bakugan, cards, BP, rank points, enemy nerfs, and Stat boosters
- **Enemy decks**: Enemy decks can be randomized in the options.
- **Starter decks**: written to decks 1 and 2 after all items are in memory.
- **Difficulty Settings**: Enemies can have increased or decreased stats. At high difficulty, enemy nerf items are in the pool. The 4 nerf items together remove 50% of G-Power and 5 movement points per enemy Bakugan.

# How To Use
1. Install Dolphin Emulator
2. Download the latest release of this Bakugan AP
3. Drop 'bakugan_bb.apworld' in your 'custom_worlds' folder
4. After your multiworld is created, open 'BakuganAP.exe' and enter your player name and port
5. Press "Refresh Inventory" to update your inventory with your AP Bakugan and cards
6. Press "Write Starter Decks" to update your premade decks
7. Play through like any AP title!
